10/07/2026 Committee Meeting Summary

The latest update from the first committee meeting of the new 2026/27 committee.

The management committee meet throughout the year to discuss how we can improve the club, make decisions and assign actions. A big thank you to those who put forward topics, it’s a great way for us to find problems and make improvements that we may not have thought of. We aim to have the next meeting around 6-8 weeks from the last one (late Aug/early Sept 2026).

Agenda

🤜🤛 New committee welcomes

Introduced the new committee members to the committee and discussed the things that they’re responsible for.

📢 Safeguarding Documentation Updates

We have decided to make updates to the safeguarding and welfare processes, some changes include:

  • Reviewing safeguarding policies
  • Updating the complaints and disciplinary policy
  • Allowing people to find contact information for safeguarding and welfare for confidential issues if they do not have their details already
  • Releasing a medical declaration form

🥇 Improving interest in regionals

We discussed how we can get more teams entered into regional competitions and opens. We hope entering more open competitions will provide lower-stakes opportunities whilst still scratching that competetive itch. We’re gauging interest on which competitions people want to enter and competetive signup forms will be released for Nationals, Regionals, Opens, Foam competitions shortly.

☀️ Summer Recruitment Plans

A marketing and outreach plan has been drafted and will be enacted this summer which will include: attending Reading Pride, contacting ‘What’s on Reading’ and other local outlets.

✍️ Committee & Club methodology

We plan to draft an update to the club constitution, which will be voted on by members at an EGM.

💻 Website updates

We want to update the site to make it a better landing page for the club.

🏆 Womens LPS

When Women’s Court is held we’ll end with a Women’s Last Player Standing (LPS) which will be tracked as Tuesday and Thursday LPS with its own trophy too.

🥳 Volunteer appreciation

The committee would like a way to show appreciation for members of the club that go above and beyond. We have a few ideas and will be announcing it once finalised.

📝 Feedback form

Feedback from the club greatly helps us prioritise topics that we have on the agenda so thank you everyone that contributed. We go through every suggestion submitted to the club, anything we didn’t have time to discuss will be brought up at the next committee meeting. Here’s a rundown of some of the feedback given by the club:

Foam - more please?

We love having foam sessions and want to see what frequency works for the club. Interest will be gathered in a training feedback form.

Who are the new committee?

For those unaware we have a new committee since the AGM!

We’ve updated committee members roles in the Facebook and Whatsapp groups to make it easier to see who is who. And the up to date members of committee are permanently on the meet the committee page.

How do we improve the competetive culture of the club?

The committee discussed whether the competetive culture has become negative. While competition is important, we are also a social club. We agreed that different coaching/training practices, positive attitudes towards other teams, and creating more supportive environments will help.

More non-nationals meets!

This was something the committee wanted to discuss and it’s good to see the club agree.

More team-lead coaching

The coaching training structure has been a great addition last season. We want to provide more oppurtunities for non-coaches to pitch in with coaching/training/feedback in general.

More summer socials

Summer is upon us and we have some great weather, we plan to take advantage of that for some summer socials. Watch this space.

New kit designs

With the majority of comp players now having the current kit, recently changed <2 years ago if a new kit was introduced we do not believe it would be fair to ask everyone to buy it to play in. There is not an appetite for another kit right now.
